# DecorativePageBackground

## Responsibility

Repeats the shared decorative wallpaper accent at fixed positions on content pages (top-right, mid-left rotated, bottom-right).

## When to use

Use on long or policy-style pages that should share the brand wallpaper treatment.

## When not to use

Do not use on full-bleed media pages (e.g. homepage hero) or pages that already have a strong visual background.

## Behaviour

- Renders three non-interactive pieces behind page content.
- Mid piece is rotated 180° and sits further down on the left.
- Bottom piece sits near the end of the page on the right.
